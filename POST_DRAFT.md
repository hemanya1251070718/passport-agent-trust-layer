### LinkedIn Post Draft

Excited to showcase what we built for **Homingo Hack Days (Edition 01)** in the Agents track: **Passport: The Agent Trust & Control Layer** 🚀

The internet was built for humans. When AI agents run errands on the web, they face two critical issues:
1. They have no safe, revocable identity without giving away raw human credentials.
2. Web content scraped during autonomous research can contain adversarial prompt injections that coerce agents to drain wallets or exfiltrate private data.

We designed Passport as an "Okta + Stripe Limits + Flight Recorder" middleware for autonomous agents:
- **Cryptographic Delegation**: Ed25519 signed delegation tokens giving agents strict scopes and spending caps.
- **Governed Web Research**: Powered by **Olostep**, our agent investigates vendor refund terms with zero risk of malicious prompt injection taking over the execution context.
- **Vendor Risk Intelligence**: Powered by **Crustdata**, vetting merchants before any wallet spend is settled.
- **Adversarial Red-Team Benchmark**: Evaluated across 60 scenarios with a **100% attack block rate** and **0.0% false positive rate**.

Check out the public repository and live benchmark:
